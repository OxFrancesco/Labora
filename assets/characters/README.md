# Character references

These transparent PNGs are image-generation concepts from 2026-10-02. The yellow star established the face and proportions; the other shapes used it as a reference. Material studies are in `material-references/`. Earlier folders retain their exact prompts in `prompts.json`. `cute-reference/` contains the later proportions and material study, with its exact prompt in `prompt.json`.

The app uses the actual Blender models in [../characters3d](../characters3d/README.md). These concept images are not packaged as runtime avatars. Their bodies, eyes, highlights, and smiles were rebuilt as editable geometry with distinct materials.
